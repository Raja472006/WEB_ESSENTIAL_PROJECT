Features

Everything You Need for Daily Nutrition

Calorie Tracking

Log daily meals across Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks. Watch real-time calorie budgets update with instant progress indicators.

Macronutrient Monitoring

Track protein, carbohydrates, and fats in grams and percentages to balance muscle repair, energy levels, and healthy cellular function.

Food History

Browse your daily, weekly, or 30-day nutrition history. Inspect meal breakdowns and analyze long-term nutritional patterns.

Personal Goals

Customize target calories, macronutrient targets, and daily water goals tailored to your individual health journey.

Water Tracking

Interactive visual water glass counter with one-click increment and decrement controls to keep your body properly hydrated.

Interactive Nutrition Charts

Powered by Chart.js. Visualize daily macronutrient ratios and weekly calorie curves rendered directly from database queries.

Academic Project Overview

NutriTrack – Web Essentials Project

NutriTrack is an academic full-stack web application engineered for the college Web Essentials mini-project. It integrates modern responsive frontend engineering with a RESTful Express.js backend, relational database persistence, salted bcrypt password hashing, stateless JWT authorization, and dynamic Chart.js visualizations.
